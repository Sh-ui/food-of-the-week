## Sunday Batch

##### BatchDay
###### 1:00 stock + 1:15 bath + 3:30 store

About two hours of hands while the pot and the bath run themselves.

- [ ] Chicken parts, onion, carrot ends, peppercorns. Bare simmer *3 hours*. Strain and jar.

### Bath
- [ ] Chicken breasts, two bags (Tuesday, lunches), *150F*, *90 min*.
- [ ] Bump to *183F*. Whole baby potatoes *45 min*; white sweet potato chunks *35 min*. Oil and salt only.

## Meatloafs

##### Prep&Heat
###### 5:00 prep + 5:15 bake

#### Anchor:
Mini **meatloafs** -- ground beef, grated onion, parsley, egg, breadcrumbs. Ketchup glaze, quick gravy from the homemade stock.

#### Fan:
Sunday's whole **baby potatoes**, stir-fried whole in a hot wok with garlic and scallion.

#### Cai:
**Carrot cabbage slaw** with a cider-mustard dressing.

**Peas**, boiled, butter and salt.

### Already Prepped
- Potatoes cooked and sealed, slaw base, *2 cups* stock.

### Sous Chef - Prep (5:00)
- [ ] Mix and shape *10* small loaves, brush with ketchup. Oven *400F*, in at *5:15*, *25-30 min*.
- [ ] Dress *5 cups* slaw: cider vinegar, Dijon, honey, oil, salt.
- [ ] Slice *3* garlic cloves and *2* scallions. Potato bag out of the fridge.
- [ ] Boil the peas *3 min*.

### Chef - Cooking
Pat the potatoes dry, wok them whole until blistered, garlic and scallion for the last *30 seconds*. Gravy from the pan drippings, a spoon of flour and the stock. ***Taste*** for salt and a splash of vinegar.

**Table**: meatloafs on a board, gravy beside, potatoes, slaw, peas.

## Coconut Chicken

##### SousVidePrep
###### 5:00 prep + 5:30 rice

#### Anchor:
Sunday's **chicken breast**, seared for color and sliced, with **white sweet potato** browned in the same pan and a coconut milk and stock glaze reduced around it. Not a curry.

#### Fan:
Jasmine rice.

#### Cai:
**Fresh pineapple relish** -- pineapple, red onion, jalapeno, cilantro, lemon. Made tonight so it stays bright; part topping, part side.

**Smashed cucumber** with lemon and crushed peanuts.

### Already Prepped
- Chicken and sweet potato bags from Sunday, *1 cup* stock, cut pineapple.

### Sous Chef - Prep (5:00)
- [ ] Relish: *2 cups* pineapple diced small, *1/2* red onion, *1* jalapeno, cilantro, juice of *1* lemon, salt.
- [ ] Smash and salt *1* English cucumber. Crush the peanuts.
- [ ] *5:30* start the rice. Dress the cucumbers.

### Chef - Cooking
Pat everything dry. Sweet potato cut-side down until deep brown. Chicken *90 seconds* a side, color only. *1/2 can* coconut milk plus the stock, reduce hard to a glaze. ***Taste*** for salt and lemon.

**Table**: skillet in the middle, relish with a spoon, cucumbers, rice.

## Sausage Orzo

##### EarlyHold
###### 3:45 start + 4:45 table

**Mom cooks this one solo.** One pot, no tomato, on the table at 4:45 before dance. Holds with the lid on.

#### Anchor:
**Italian sausage** browned with onion and the fine carrot cabbage shred, **orzo** toasted and simmered in the homemade stock, finished with lemon, frozen peas and parmesan.

#### Fan:
The orzo, in the pot.

#### Cai:
**Marinated white beans** from Sunday.

**Bag salad.**

### Already Prepped
- *5 cups* stock, *2 cups* fine shred, white beans.

### Sous Chef - Prep (3:45)
- [ ] Dice *1* onion, squeeze the sausage from its casings, measure *2 cups* orzo, zest and juice *1* lemon.

### Chef - Cooking (4:00, all mom)
Brown the sausage. Onion and shred *5 min*. Orzo in, toast *1 min*. *4 cups* stock, simmer *10-12 min*, last cup if it dries. Off heat: *1.5 cups* peas, lemon, parmesan. ***Taste***. Lid on.

**Table**: pot on a trivet, beans, salad, parmesan.

## Gringo Tostadas

##### SousChefDish
###### 5:00 lentils + 5:20 prep

#### Anchor:
**Taco lentils** simmered in stock with onion and garlic, piled on tostadas.

#### Fan:
Tostadas.

#### Cai:
**Slaw** with lemon, cilantro and a can of corn.

**Refried beans** baked with cheese on top.

### Already Prepped
- *4 cups* stock, slaw base.

### Sous Chef - Prep (5:00)
- [ ] Soften *1* onion and *3* garlic cloves, *2 tbsp* taco seasoning, *2 cups* lentils, the stock. Simmer *25-30 min* until thick.
- [ ] *5:20* slaw: *5 cups* shred, drained corn, *1* lemon, cilantro, oil, salt.
- [ ] Refried beans into a dish, cheese over, *375F* about *15 min*.
- [ ] **Move the bagged pork loin from the freezer to the fridge.**

### Chef - Cooking
***Taste*** the lentils for salt, lemon and heat. Warm the tostadas *3 min*.

**Table**: lentils, beans, slaw, tostadas, cheese, lemon wedges, hot sauce.
