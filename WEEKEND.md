# Weekend - Saturday, August 22, 2026

> The two ButcherBox cuts that never got cooked off the last stock-up: ribs and the whole chicken are still in the deep freezer three weeks later. Chicken waits for another weekend -- it needs its own carving attention and this one is already full. It's only a half rack of ribs, so potatoes and white beans go in the same pan underneath, catching all the fat and glaze -- one pan, twice as filling, no extra dish. Sunday is salmon plus the week's real work: the pork loin gets a simple herb rub and vacuum sealed (not cooked) so it's ready to sous vide straight from the bag Thursday, and the mushrooms, onion, and cabbage for the rest of the week get chopped and bagged while the oven's already going. Everything below is an Aldi list -- nothing specialty.

## Baby Back Ribs & Pan Beans

##### SlowOven
###### now thaw + 1:00 rub + 2:00 oven

#### Anchor:
**Baby back ribs** (half rack), rubbed and foil-wrapped, baked *low* directly on top of a bed of **potatoes** and **white beans** so the rendered fat and glaze baste them all afternoon. Opened and glazed hard with **BBQ sauce**, **brown sugar**, and **cider vinegar**, then the potatoes and beans finish uncovered alongside for the same blast of heat -- one pan, one meal, the sides cook in the ribs' own drippings.

#### Cai:
**Coleslaw** -- shredded cabbage, mayo, cider vinegar, a little sugar, celery seed if you've got it, salt and pepper.

**Cucumber onion salad** -- thin-sliced cucumber and onion, cider vinegar, sugar, salt, a crack of black pepper. Classic deli-style, cold and sharp.

### Already Prepped

- Nothing yet -- check the ribs first, before anything else today.

### Sous Chef - Prep (whenever, no rush)

- [ ] **Check if the ribs are thawed.** If they are still hard in the middle, submerge the sealed pack in a bowl of cold water, weighted with a plate. *3-4 hours*, changing the water once if it gets warm.
- [ ] Mix the rub: *3 tbsp* **brown sugar**, *1 tbsp* **smoked paprika**, *2 tsp* **cumin**, *1 tbsp* **salt**, *2 tsp* **black pepper**, *1 tsp* **garlic powder**.
- [ ] Halve or quarter *1.5 lb* **baby potatoes** so the pieces are all roughly the same size.
- [ ] Drain and rinse *2 cans* **white beans**.
- [ ] Shred *half* the **cabbage** thin into a slaw bowl. **Half and stop** -- the rest keeps for Thursday.
- [ ] Slice *1* **cucumber** and *half* a **yellow onion** thin into a second bowl.

### Sous Chef - Assembly (whenever)

- [ ] Dress the slaw: *1/3 cup* **mayo**, *2 tbsp* **cider vinegar**, *1 tbsp* **sugar**, **salt**, **pepper**. Refrigerate -- better after an hour.
- [ ] Dress the cucumber and onion: *3 tbsp* **cider vinegar**, *1 tsp* **sugar**, **salt**. Refrigerate at least an hour.
- [ ] Toss the **potatoes** and **beans** with olive oil, salt, and a spoon of the smoked paprika rub. Spread in a layer across a large sheet pan.
- [ ] Mix the glaze: *1/2 cup* **BBQ sauce**, *2 tbsp* **brown sugar**, *1 tbsp* **cider vinegar**.

### Chef - Cooking (whenever the oven's free)

Pull the membrane off the bone side of the **ribs** -- work a knife under it at one end, grab it with a paper towel, and peel.

Rub both sides hard. Wrap the rack tight in a double layer of foil, seam up.

Lay the foil-wrapped ribs directly on top of the potato-and-bean layer on the sheet pan.

*275F*, *3 hours*. No peeking. Ribs are done when a toothpick slides into the meat between bones with no resistance; the potatoes will be soft and glossy with rendered fat underneath.

Lift the ribs off, pour off the liquid, brush the top heavy with the **glaze**. Stir the potatoes and beans where they sit -- they've caught all the drippings.

Both back in *uncovered* at *425F* for *10-15 min* -- ribs to tack up the glaze, potatoes and beans to crisp at the edges. Watch the glaze -- sugar goes from caramel to carbon fast.

Rest the ribs *10 min* before cutting between the bones.

**Table**: potatoes and beans straight from the pan into a serving dish, ribs on a board over them or alongside. Coleslaw and cucumber salad in bowls, plenty of napkins.

## Sunday Batch Prep

Do this earlier in the day, ahead of the salmon dinner -- it is the thing that makes Monday through Thursday easy.

### Pork Loin - Rub & Vacuum Seal (for Thursday's sous vide)

Not cooked today. It sits and seasons in the bag over four days, and the actual cook happens Thursday straight from the fridge.

- [ ] **Move the pork loin roast to the fridge Saturday night if it's not already thawing.** It needs to be fully thawed to season and seal today.
- [ ] Pat the **pork loin** dry. Rub all over with olive oil, *3* minced **garlic** cloves, *1 tbsp* chopped **thyme** (or *1 tsp* dried), **salt**, **black pepper**.
- [ ] Vacuum seal the rubbed pork loin. Refrigerate flat -- do not freeze, do not cook. It sits until Thursday.

### Prep for the Week

- [ ] Slice **8 oz mushrooms** for Monday, bagged.
- [ ] Mince *8* **garlic** cloves total, split across small containers for Monday and Tuesday.
- [ ] Dice *1* **yellow onion** for Monday, bagged.
- [ ] Dice *1* more **yellow onion** for Wednesday, bagged.
- [ ] Shred the **other half** of Saturday's cabbage for Thursday's apple slaw, bagged undressed (it dresses better fresh Thursday).
- [ ] Scrub *2 lb* **potatoes** for Thursday's mash, bagged in the fridge -- no need to peel yet.
- [ ] Trim *1 lb* **green beans** for Tuesday, bagged.

## Salmon Sheet Pan

##### SheetPan
###### 4:45 prep + 5:15 oven

#### Anchor:
**Salmon** fillets, lemon-garlic-butter, roasted on a sheet pan with **green beans** alongside -- one pan, one temperature, done in twenty minutes.

#### Cai:
**Sliced tomato** -- roma tomatoes, olive oil, salt, torn basil if you have it.

**Buttered corn** -- a can or bag of corn, butter, salt, pepper. Two minutes, no real cooking.

### Already Prepped

- Nothing -- salmon and green beans for tonight come straight off the sheet pan.

### Sous Chef - Prep (4:45)

- [ ] Move the sealed **salmon** to a bowl of cold water. *20-30 min* to thaw fully.
- [ ] Slice *3* **roma tomatoes** onto a plate.
- [ ] Trim *1 lb* **green beans** (save half of Saturday's trimmed bag for Tuesday if there's extra).
- [ ] Line a sheet pan with foil.

### Sous Chef - Assembly (5:15)

- [ ] Start the **white rice** in the rice cooker.
- [ ] Pat the **salmon** dry, lay on the sheet pan.
- [ ] Toss the **green beans** in olive oil and salt, arrange around the salmon.
- [ ] Melt *3 tbsp* **butter** with *2* minced **garlic** cloves and the juice of *half a lemon*, brush over the salmon.
- [ ] Dress the tomatoes: olive oil, salt, torn basil.
- [ ] Oven to *425F*.

### Chef - Cooking (when home, or mom finishes solo -- no judgment calls, just time)

Salmon and green beans in at *425F*, *12-15 min*, until the salmon flakes and the beans are blistered.

Warm the **corn** in a small pot with butter, salt, and pepper, *2-3 min*.

**Table**: sheet pan straight to a trivet, rice in a bowl, tomatoes and corn alongside.
