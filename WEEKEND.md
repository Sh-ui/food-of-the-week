# Weekend - Saturday, August 8, 2026

> The two ButcherBox cuts that will not fit a weeknight. Ribs go in low on Saturday afternoon and the oven does the work; the whole chicken roasts Sunday. Both are long on time and short on attention.

## Baby Back Ribs

##### Prep&Heat+
###### 8:00am defrost + 1:00 rub + 2:00 oven

#### Anchor:
**Baby back ribs** rubbed with brown sugar, smoked paprika, and cumin, wrapped tight in foil and baked *low* for three hours, then opened and glazed hard under high heat.

#### Fan:
Buttered rice, or bread. Whatever is around.

#### Cai:
**Vinegar slaw** -- the rest of the week's cabbage, shredded, with cider vinegar, sugar, and black pepper. No mayonnaise; it has to cut the fat.

**Cucumber and onion** -- thin-sliced, salted, left in vinegar an hour.

### Already Prepped

- **Ribs** thawed in the fridge overnight Friday.

### Sous Chef - Prep (whenever, no rush)

- [ ] Mix the rub: *3 tbsp* **brown sugar**, *1 tbsp* **smoked paprika**, *2 tsp* **cumin**, *1 tbsp* **salt**, *2 tsp* **black pepper**, *1 tsp* **garlic powder**.
- [ ] Shred the remaining **cabbage** thin into a bowl.
- [ ] Slice *1* **cucumber** and *half* a **yellow onion** paper thin into a second bowl.
- [ ] Dress the cucumber and onion: *3 tbsp* **cider vinegar**, *1 tsp* **sugar**, **salt**. Refrigerate at least an hour.

### Chef - Cooking

Pull the membrane off the bone side of the **ribs** -- work a knife under it at one end, grab it with a paper towel, and peel. Skipping this is why ribs come out chewy.

Rub both sides hard. Wrap the rack tight in a double layer of foil, seam up, on a sheet pan.

*275F*, *3 hours*. No peeking. They are done when a toothpick slides into the meat between bones with no resistance.

Open the foil, pour off the liquid, and brush the top with a heavy glaze -- **barbecue sauce**, or ketchup cut with vinegar, brown sugar, and smoked paprika.

Back in *uncovered* at *425F* for *10-15 min*, until the glaze is tacky and blistered at the edges. Watch it; sugar goes from caramel to carbon fast.

Rest *10 min* before cutting between the bones.

**Table**: ribs on a board, both slaws in bowls, plenty of napkins.

## Roast Chicken

##### Prep&Heat
###### 4:00 salt + 5:00 oven

#### Anchor:
The **whole chicken**, salted early and roasted breast-up at high heat on a bed of **onion** and **carrot**. The vegetables underneath catch the fat and become the side dish.

#### Fan:
The pan vegetables, plus bread to mop.

#### Cai:
**Lemon parsley salad** -- whatever greens and parsley are left, dressed with lemon and olive oil at the last second.

**Sliced tomato** -- olive oil, salt.

### Already Prepped

- **Chicken** thawed in the fridge -- it needs *two full days* from the deep freezer, so it moves Thursday or Friday.

### Sous Chef - Prep (4:00)

- [ ] Cut *2* **yellow onions** into thick wedges into a roasting pan.
- [ ] Cut *3* **carrots** into big chunks into the same pan.
- [ ] Toss the vegetables with **olive oil** and **salt**.
- [ ] Slice *3* **roma tomatoes** onto a plate, dress with **olive oil** and **salt**.

### Chef - Cooking

Pat the **chicken** bone dry inside and out. Salt it heavily all over, including inside the cavity, and leave it uncovered on the counter while the oven heats. Dry skin is the whole game.

Set the bird breast-up directly on the **onion and carrot**. Oil and pepper over the skin.

*425F*, *70-90 min*, until the thickest part of the thigh reads *165F* and the juices at the joint run clear.

Rest *15 min* on a board before carving. Skipping the rest costs you all the juice.

Tip any resting juices back into the pan vegetables and stir.

**Table**: carved chicken on the board, the pan vegetables in their own dish, salad and tomatoes alongside. Save the carcass -- it is a pot of stock.
